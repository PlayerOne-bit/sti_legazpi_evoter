Features

Everything an election needs, in one app

Students get a fast, clear ballot. Organizers get seasons, candidates, voter lists and results without paper or spreadsheets.

For students

A ballot that guides you

One position per page with a step bar on top. Pick a candidate, review every choice on one screen, then submit.

Live countdowns

See which seasons are voting now and which open next, right on the sign-in screen. Times come from the server, not your phone's clock.

One password per season

Each election has its own password, emailed to your school address. Signing in opens only the season it belongs to.

Results right away

After you vote, follow the tally. When the season closes, winners and full polls appear instantly.

Light and dark mode

Follows your device, or switch it yourself. Works on phones held upright or sideways, and on PCs.

Updates itself

When a new version is out, the sign-in screen offers an update. No need to come back to this page.

For organizers

Seasons on a calendar

Pick the voting days on one calendar. Days used by another season are marked in red, so elections never overlap.

Candidates and party lists

Add positions and candidates in one step, with cropped photos, party list logos and colors, and ballot order by rank.

Voter lists and email

Every season builds its voter list by grade level. Send each student their password by email, one at a time or all at once.

Excel in, Excel out

Import students from a spreadsheet; returning students are updated for the new school year. Export voter lists and results.

Live turnout, instant tally

Watch turnout climb on the dashboard. Close the season and the winners are counted and saved to History.

Built-in safeguards

Seasons lock once voting starts, admin roles with a super admin, optional two-step sign-in, and limits on sign-in attempts.

How it works

From setup to winners in four steps

  1. 1

    Organizers set up a season

    Club, school year, voting days, who can vote, and the candidates for each position.

  2. 2

    Students get their password

    Each student receives a password for that season at lastname.XXXXXX@legazpi.sti.edu.ph.

  3. 3

    Vote while it's open

    Sign in with your student number and that password, pick one candidate per position, and submit.

  4. 4

    Results the moment it closes

    The tally is counted on the server and the winners are shown to everyone right away.

Guide

Using STI eVoter

Short answers for students and organizers. Tap a question to open it.

Students

How do I sign in?

Choose Student on the sign-in screen, enter your student number, and the season password. The password is emailed to lastname.XXXXXX@legazpi.sti.edu.ph, where XXXXXX is the last 6 digits of your student number. Your adviser can also give it to you.

Why doesn't last season's password work?

Every season has its own password, and signing in opens only the season it belongs to. Use the newest password you received.

How do I vote?

Pick one candidate for each position, then tap Next. On the last page, review your choices and tap Submit ballot. You can vote once per season, and only while it's open.

Is my vote secret?

Yes. The server records that you voted separately from what you chose. Ballots carry no student number, so nobody can match a ballot to a student.

It says I'm not on the voter list.

Each season is open to certain grade levels only. If you should be able to vote, ask the organizers to check your grade level and that your record is active for this school year.

When can I see the results?

After you vote you can follow the live tally. The final winners appear as soon as the organizers close the season.

Organizers

How do I run an election?

Sign in as Admin. In Seasons, tap Create voting season, choose the club, school year, voting days and grade levels. Then open Candidates on the season card and add each candidate; create positions right there with New position.

How do students get their passwords?

Open Voters on the season card. Send passwords by email to everyone at once or one by one, make a new password for a student, or export the list to Excel to print.

Can I change a season after voting starts?

No. Once voting opens the season is locked so the rules can't change mid-vote. To end it early, use Close season and tally votes. A season that still has no candidates when voting opens is removed automatically.

How do I add students?

In Students, add them one by one or import an Excel file (download the template from the Excel menu). Student numbers and full names must be unique. At the start of each school year, last year's students become inactive until they are imported again.

Where are past elections?

In History, with winners and full polls. Run again copies a season's setup into a new one for the next school year.

Troubleshooting

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

That's SmartScreen, shown for apps that aren't sold in a store. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Android won't install the APK.

Allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps when Android asks. If an older copy came from somewhere else, uninstall it first.

"Cannot reach the server."

Check your internet connection. The first sign-in after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up; try again.

How do I update the app?

Open the app. When a new version is out, the sign-in screen shows Update. You can also download it again here; your data lives on the server, so nothing is lost.
